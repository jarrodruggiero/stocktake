{# Rendered always and hidden by script, not injected by it: with JavaScript off this is simply visible, which is the safe way round for a warning. The server refuses this combination with a 400 either way — the notice exists so nobody discovers that by clicking Download. #}

A CSV file holds one sheet. “Everything” is nine reports, so it needs Excel — one workbook, one sheet per report. Choose Excel, or pick a single report to get a CSV of it.

What's in each

ReportContents
All trades Every buy, sell and DRP allocation — the raw ledger, in AUD and native currency.
Current holdings Open positions: units, average price, cost, market value, gain and dividends.
Dividends and franking Each payment with its franking credits and the grossed-up figure, tagged by financial year.
Realised capital gains Sales matched FIFO to the parcels they consumed: cost base, proceeds, gross gain, the 50% discount where the parcel was held over a year, and the gain after it.
Unrealised capital gains The same view for parcels you still hold — what selling today would realise.
Performance by financial year Invested, value and gain at each 30 June, plus the change within each year.
Closed positions One row per instrument fully exited, with capital gain before and after the discount and total return including dividends.
